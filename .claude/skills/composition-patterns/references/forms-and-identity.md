# Forms & identity flows

The `ui-form-provider` + `ui-field` spine and its nine consumer rows: the base labelled form,
then the ADR-0176 identity & account family (credential sign-in, magic link, OTP, social
sign-in, onboarding, account-settings), plus two GH #960 host-seam/verdict rows (passkey,
password-strength) — all ride the same registry/labelling seam. Read this file when wiring any
form, or any of the six auth/onboarding/account surfaces. The first four identity rows are ALSO
the sign-in/registration composition recipe (GH #960): each maps 1:1 onto its own
`.claude/docs/flows/*.flow.json` journey card — the exemplar page realizes exactly that card's
states/exits, nothing more, nothing less.
