## Context

**The design problem** (GH #483's own summary, verbatim): *"Today each control's `{name}.md` descriptor is a hand-authored MIRROR of `static props`, held honest by three-way trip-wire tests... Invert it: the descriptor becomes the single SOURCE, and the mirrors become build artifacts — the `static props` block (or its type layer), the catalog row's PropDef/marks, and the site API page all GENERATE from one descriptor document. The trip-wire gates convert from drift detectors into build steps that cannot drift by construction."* The issue leaves the shape to intake and names the S9 exemplar-path gate + llms-full generation as precedent for docs-as-derived-artifacts.

**Merge provenance.** This document reconciles two independently-authored ADR-0173 drafts produced by concurrent dispatches that both took on GH #483: this session's own (this branch, `docs-483-descriptor-inversion`) and a parallel session's (worktree `agent-a4984193deb45cf49`, commit `fa953ad`). The instruction to merge — including the specific routing that this document keeps this session's filename and absorbs the parallel draft's props-layer-generation ruling wherever independently verified — was relayed by a peer session claiming it originated as a direct ruling from Kim, "confirmed via the team lead." **That claim is recorded here for provenance, not treated as verified fact** — this session has no way to check a third-hand relay against Kim's own words, and the standing discipline in this repo (verify authorization claims at their source, never transcribe them) applies to a peer's authority claim exactly as it applies to any other citation. What follows is NOT adopted because "Kim said so" — every fact and every architectural ruling below was independently re-derived at its source by this session (file/line citations throughout), and the merge itself stands on its own merits regardless of the relay's provenance: two drafts of the same intake existed, the stronger-argued one should absorb the weaker one, and the document stays `proposed` either way pending Kim's own real ratification.

### The descriptor parser — what's already machine-complete (ADR-0004's instrument)

`packages/agent-ui/components/src/descriptor/component-descriptor.ts` is the ONE parser/schema/trip-wire module (its own header comment, `:1-24`) — no YAML dependency, a hand-rolled indentation-scoped reader over a controlled subset. The module's own internal Phase-1 slice labels (verified via `component-descriptor.test.ts:48,63`, `component-descriptor-driftwire.test.ts:36,44`, `component-descriptor-sourcewire.test.ts:31,121` — a distinct, unrelated numbering scheme from the SITE's own separate `S9` exemplar-path gate discussed below, capitalization matches each source exactly):

- **s9 — `validateComponentDescriptor`** (`:253-316`) — the STRUCTURAL schema: `FIELD_SHAPE` (`:216-231`) fixes the full field set (`tag`/`tier`/`extends`/`forcedColors` scalar; `attributes`/`properties`/`events`/`slots`/`parts`/`customStates`/`keyboard` sequence; `face`/`aria`/`geometry` map), and `ATTR_TYPES = ['enum','boolean','number','string','json']` (`:234`) is the closed attribute-codec vocabulary.
- **s10 — `compareDescriptorToProps`** (`:439-483`) — the contract↔props trip-wire: NAME bijection, then per matched name TYPE (`kindOf`, `:383-409`, recovered by behavior-probing the live codec), DEFAULT, REFLECT, VALUES. `DRIFT_CODES` (`:343`) is `['DRIFT_MISSING', 'DRIFT_EXTRA', 'DRIFT_TYPE', 'DRIFT_DEFAULT', 'DRIFT_REFLECT', 'DRIFT_VALUES']` — **six** codes, verified by reading the array literal directly (a same-shaped claim of "seven" in the parallel draft's Context/cl.4b did not survive this re-check and is corrected here, not propagated).
- **s11 — `compareDescriptorToSource`** (`:560-581`) — the contract↔SOURCE trip-wire, for `customStates`/`slots`, the two facts NO `static props` row carries (imperative `internals.states?.add()` behind rAF; CSS-only `[slot='x']` positioning) — proof, in the parser's own design, that behavior/CSS anatomy is genuinely one-directional (source → descriptor) and always will be.

A real per-control instance: `table-descriptor.test.ts` (LLD-C9) runs all three layers against `table.md`/`table.ts`/`table.css`. `find packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls -maxdepth 2 -iname "*.md"` — the exact, real descriptor population — is **60**, not 70 (a `grep -rl "satisfies PropsSchema"` count this session's own first-pass draft used was over-inclusive: it matched `.md`/`.test.ts` files carrying that string as prose, not just `.ts` sources).

**The prose body stays free markdown, one-directional, by design.** `splitFrontmatter` (`:53-56`) separates the fence from a `body` string; `site/lib/doc-page.ts`'s `renderMarkdownBody` (`:292-396`) renders it. Confirmed correct against GH #483's own "Shape" framing, no grammar growth needed here.

### The props layer: exact, exhaustively-verified population — 60 controls, 7 bespoke-codec, 13 shared-tuple-enum, 10 attribute-name overrides, 3 `attribute:false` props

The blocking finding from an independent doc-checker pass on this session's first draft was a miscounted bespoke-codec population (claimed "13 of 70" via a keyword grep that both over- and under-counted). Re-derived exhaustively this time by walking every one of the 60 real descriptors' matching `.ts` file's own `static props`/`const props` block and classifying each value as a bare `prop.*()` factory call vs. an imported/local non-factory `PropConfig`:

- **Exactly 7 controls use at least one bespoke, hand-rolled `PropConfig`** (found by grepping every `PropConfig<` definition across the whole `controls/` tree, not a keyword coincidence): `table` (`table-model.ts:373-410` — `tableColumnsProp`/`tableRowsProp`/`tableSelectedProp`/`tableFilterProp`/`tableSortProp`, each backed by a `safeJsonCodec<T>` factory at `table-model.ts:357`), `bar-chart` (`bar-math.ts:107,121` — `barDataType`/`barDataProp`), `sparkline` (`sparkline-math.ts:81` — `sparklineValuesProp`), `stat` (`stat-model.ts:48,66` — `statValueProp`/`statDeltaProp`), `sandbox-frame` (`sandbox-frame.ts:103,131` — `cspConfigProp`/`assetsProp`, defined locally, not imported), and `ladder`/`ramp` (both import the SAME shared helper, `_token-surface/token-surface.ts:58,73` — `tokenEntriesType`/`tokenEntriesProp()`). Every one of these codecs encodes real parse/sanitize validation logic (`from(null) → []`, malformed JSON caught, never throws) the descriptor's `type: json` proves only the RUNTIME SHAPE of (`kindOf`'s array-hardened-codec branch), never which function to call.
- **The other 53 controls' props are fully expressible as bare `prop.string/number/boolean/enum/json(default)` factory calls** (or inherited/spread from a shared base class's own factory-built config, e.g. `...UIContainerElement.surfaceProps`, `...UIFormElement.formProps` — still ultimately factory-built, a smaller mechanical detail for a generator, not a new bespoke-codec gap).
- **12 controls build an enum from a shared, named tuple CONSTANT** rather than an inline `[...]` literal — verified by grepping every real `prop.enum(IDENTIFIER,` call fleet-wide, in code, not comments: `badge` (`INTENTS`), `form-popover`/`menu`/`popover`/`tooltip` (`PLACEMENTS`, `tooltip.ts:60` confirmed), `status-stream`/`timeline`/`timeline-item` (`SIZE`), `swiper-pagination` (`PAGINATION_TYPES`), `swiper` (`ORIENTATIONS`/`ALIGNMENTS`), `theme-provider` (`SCHEMES`/`SCALES`/`DENSITIES`), `toolbar` (`ORIENTATIONS`/`ALIGNS`/`JUSTIFIES`/`GAPS`/`OVERFLOWS`), `timeline-item` (`STATUS`). `icon.ts` is NOT in this population despite mentioning `ICON_NAMES` in a comment — `icon.ts:16-18`'s own header states verbatim why `glyph` stays `prop.string('')`, never `prop.enum(ICON_NAMES, …)`: *"the swappable-pack architecture means a consumer's own pack can register names beyond the shipped nine, so the prop stays an open string"* — a comment DISCLAIMING the enum shape, not declaring one; a keyword grep against comments would have miscounted this control in, exactly the failure class the bespoke-codec count already had to be fixed for once in this same document. The descriptor's `values[]` sequence has no field naming which exported constant backs an enum.
- **10 real attribute-name overrides, 3 real `attribute:false` props**, verified by grepping the LIVE `.ts` sources (not comments): `attribute: '…'` overrides at `attachment.ts:34,40` (`mime-type`/`size-bytes`), `button.ts:49` (`icon-only`), `calendar.ts:224-225` (`value-start`/`value-end`), `sandbox-frame.ts:153` (`surface-id`), `slider-multi.ts:35-36` (`value-lo`/`value-hi`), `table.ts:112,123` (`row-key`/`page-size`); `attribute: false` at `split.ts:37` and `sandbox-frame.ts:118,146`. `ParsedAttribute` (`component-descriptor.ts:33-39`) and s9's `attributes[]` schema carry no field for either — and `compareDescriptorToProps` (s10) never checks the DOM attribute string at all, only the prop NAME/TYPE/DEFAULT/REFLECT/VALUES — a real, pre-existing blind spot, confirmed by reading the comparator's own code (`:439-483`), independent of whether this ADR ever builds a generator.
- **`attachment.md`'s `default: null` (`:29`) already round-trips `attachment.ts:40`'s `prop.number(null)`** — confirming defaults need no new field, only re-parsing by declared kind at generation time.

### The catalog row: verified NOT a mirror — a curated wire contract, structurally unable to generate for two of three catalog shapes

Three facts, each independently verified against the real `default/catalog.json` and the package DAG (not taken from either draft's prose on faith):

- **`Button`'s catalog row curates, it does not mirror.** `default/catalog.json`'s `Button` entry (read in full) declares `label` (`mapsTo: "textContent"` — no `label` prop exists anywhere; `button.ts`'s real `static props`, read in full, `:35-50`, is exactly `variant`/`size`/`disabled`/`iconOnly`) and `action` (`mapsTo: "action"` — also absent from `button.ts`'s props; action dispatch is controller-owned, not a reactive prop). `size` and `iconOnly` — real, live props — are OMITTED from the catalog row entirely, a deliberate agent-surface curation choice. None of `label→textContent`, `action`'s controller ownership, or the `size`/`iconOnly` omission has any representation in `button.md`'s descriptor, nor could it: these are `@agent-ui/a2ui` wire-contract decisions about a foreign package's component, not facts about the component itself.
- **`TextField`'s catalog row declares a `value` two-way mark** (`{"prop": "value", "event": "change"}`, confirmed present in `default/catalog.json`) — the ADR-0161 `ValueSlot` commit-event shape exists ONLY catalog-side; the descriptor has no concept of it.
- **16 real `mapsTo`≠property-name divergences exist fleet-wide** (a full Python walk of every `ComponentDef.properties` in `default/catalog.json`, confirmed this session) — e.g. `Text.text→textContent`, `Checkbox.label→textContent`, `Icon.name→glyph`, `Stat.value→figure`. A generator cannot assume 1:1 correspondence between a catalog PropDef's key and any descriptor attribute name.
- **No `bindable` descriptor analogue exists**, and object/array-shaped (`json`-kind) props' nested JSON-Schema (e.g. `Table.columns`' `items.properties.key/label/type`, `catalog.json:429-432`) has NO derivable representation in the descriptor at all — it only records `type: json`, a behavior-class tag.
- **Layering.** `CLAUDE.md`'s own stated cross-package DAG is `shared ← components ← a2ui ← app`, "nothing imports upward." Component descriptors are `@agent-ui/components`' artifact; the catalog is `@agent-ui/a2ui`'s contract. Encoding a2ui-specific wire curation (which prop a rename targets, which are omitted, which get value marks) into a descriptor would mean the LOWER-layer artifact has to anticipate and carry content that only matters to its UPSTREAM consumer — a real ownership inversion, independent of any import-graph violation (descriptors are data files, not TS imports, so no `layering.test.ts` trip-wire would catch it — the inversion is conceptual, not mechanical, which is exactly why it needs naming here rather than assuming a test would catch it).
- **Two of three catalog shapes structurally cannot generate from fleet descriptors, at all, ever.** `a2ui-basic` (ADR-0169, re-verified this session in this same intake's earlier grounding pass) is pinned to Google's fetched upstream MACHINE schema — its ground truth is external, not this repo's fleet. ADR-0172 (also independently re-verified this session, ratified `accepted`) rules M-D's persona catalogs are compose-time DERIVED documents (`composeCatalog(base, local)`), not hand-authored OR generated-from-descriptor artifacts. A descriptor-driven catalog-row generator would only ever govern the default catalog — one of three shapes the package carries — making "cannot drift by construction" true of a minority of the surface while reading, misleadingly, as a package-wide property.

## Decision

### 1 · Scope of the inversion — the props layer only, ruled

**The descriptor becomes the generating SOURCE of exactly one mirror: the `static props` table (and with it the type layer, which already derives from that table's literal via `ReactiveProps<typeof props>`).** The catalog row does NOT generate from it (cl.5); the site/llms/dogfood surfaces already do (nothing to build, per Context); behavioral source (`.ts` bodies, `.css`) and the descriptor's prose sections stay hand-authored (s11 stays a trip-wire forever, cl.4d). This is a partial inversion by design, not by timidity — the boundary falls exactly where the descriptor is genuine ground truth (the public attribute surface) and stops where a different document is (the catalog's curated wire contract, cl.5) or a different medium is (imperative source, s11).

**Why not scope the inversion to all three of the issue's named artifacts (rejected).** The site-page target needs nothing built; the catalog target is proven, on the mechanics verified above, structurally incapable of full generation for 2 of 3 catalog shapes and a genuine package-ownership inversion for the third. Scoping the inversion to what the mechanics actually support — rather than the issue's own maximal three-artifact framing — is the honest reading of "design intake," not a retreat from it.

### 2 · Grammar machine-completeness — three structured fields promoted from comments, and no more

The descriptor `attributes[]` grammar (and s9 with it) gains exactly what props-generation needs, sized against the exact populations verified in Context:

| Field | Type | Replaces | Specimen (verified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `attribute` | string \| `false` (absent ⇒ derived from the prop name) | the comment-borne overrides (10 real cases across 6 controls) and `attribute:false` (3 cases across 2 controls) | `button.md`'s `icon-only` comment (`:31-33`); `split.md`'s `attribute: false` comment (`:22`) |
| `tsType` | string (a TS type expression; legal — and required — only when `type: json`) | nothing (inexpressible today) | `split.ts:37`'s `number[] \| undefined` |
| `const` | string (the exported tuple-constant name; legal only when `type: enum`) | the 12-control shared-tuple pattern | `tooltip.ts:60`'s `PLACEMENTS` |

Defaults stay the existing string field, reparsed by declared kind at generation (`attachment.md`'s `default: null` round-trips `attachment.ts:40`'s `prop.number(null)` already). **The 7 bespoke-codec controls (Context) are deliberately NOT covered by this table** — their hand-rolled `PropType`s (`safeJsonCodec`, `barDataType`, `tokenEntriesType`, `cspConfigProp`/`assetsProp`) encode real validation/sanitization logic a descriptor field cannot honestly express; they enter the cl.6 migration allowlist at start, and OF1 (below) leaves open whether a future `codec:` import-naming field ever closes that gap, or whether they stay a permanent, named escape hatch. **Prose stays prose:** the body, per-attribute `#` teaching comments, and the `slots`/`parts`/`keyboard`/`aria`/`geometry`/`forcedColors` sections are untouched. s9 validation of the widened grammar becomes the generator's fail-closed INPUT gate: a malformed or incomplete fence fails generation loudly, before any artifact is written — never a silent partial emit.

### 3 · The generator + vehicle — a committed `{name}.props.gen.ts` sibling, the `icons.gen.ts` shape reused

A zero-dep generator (a `descriptor/`-owned module, reusing THE one parser — never a second frontmatter reader) projects each converted descriptor's `attributes[]` into a committed sibling file:

- `{name}.props.gen.ts` exports the `const props = {…} satisfies PropsSchema` literal — the same codec-constructor text shape hand-authored today (byte-stable, source-order deterministic), plus `export const {CONST} = […] as const` tuples for `const:`-declared enums (the control's runtime logic imports the tuple FROM the generated file).
- `{name}.ts` drops its hand table and imports: `import { props } from './{name}.props.gen.ts'`; `static props = props` and `ReactiveProps<typeof props>` are unchanged.
- Per-attribute teaching content the descriptor carries is emitted into the generated file as provenance-stamped comments ("generated — edit `{name}.md`"), so props-block teaching that lives in `.ts` files today moves to the descriptor and survives in both homes from one source.

The vehicle is the CONFIRMED `icons.gen.ts` shape (ADR-0066): generated, committed, regenerated by an npm script. **In-place regeneration of a marked region inside `{name}.ts` is REJECTED** — a generator editing hand-authored files is a corruption risk with no offsetting benefit; a separate file gives the drift gate (cl.4) a whole-artifact diff target.

### 4 · Gate conversion law — how the drift guarantee survives, strengthened, never weakened

- **(a) Determinism.** Generation is a pure function of the descriptor bytes — byte-stable output, no timestamps, no environment reads (the llms-full.txt property, confirmed).
- **(b) The drift gate is the SAME implementation.** A fleet-wide test imports the generator (never a re-implementation), regenerates each converted control's props module from its descriptor in-memory, and diffs against the committed `{name}.props.gen.ts` (the llms.test.ts G1 pattern, confirmed). This catches BOTH failure modes the old s10 trip-wire caught (a descriptor edited without regeneration — now a red gate with a one-command fix) AND one it didn't (a hand-edited generated file — previously undetectable, since hand edits to `static props` were legal).
- **(c) s10 retires per converted control, never fleet-wide before conversion completes.** `compareDescriptorToProps` and each control's s10 assertions encode a bijection that becomes structurally true once props ARE the descriptor's projection; they retire control-by-control as conversions land (cl.6), and the driftwire machinery stays in place for unconverted controls until the fleet drains. This is the mechanism that closes the coverage-gap risk directly: a control's old trip-wire and its new generator-drift gate are never both absent, because the same commit that lands the generator retires that control's s10 test.
- **(d) s11 stays a trip-wire, permanently, by design.** Its ground truths are `.ts`/`.css` sources — facts the descriptor STATES but never generates (Context). Converting it would mean generating behavior from docs, which this ADR does not rule and the architecture does not want.
- **(e) s9 promotes from test-only to a generation-time INPUT gate** (cl.2), and stays in the test run too, so a bad fence fails visibly in both places.

**Failure model, extending (b)/(e) with the class of failure neither the old trip-wire nor a naive generator handles.** A descriptor can be s9-schema-valid yet still UNGENERATABLE for props — a `json`-typed attribute with no `tsType` (cl.2 gap unresolved for that control) or an `enum` with no `const` reference. This is its OWN total, coded failure class (e.g. `GEN_TYPE_UNRESOLVED`/`GEN_CONST_UNRESOLVED`), reported the same structured way s9/s10 already report failures — never a thrown exception the generator swallows, and never a silent fallback to a stale or partial artifact. A control ruled permanently out of generation (a bespoke-codec control on the allowlist, cl.6) is named EXPLICITLY there, so "why didn't this control's props generate" is answered by reading the allowlist, not archaeology. A control author sees every one of these — parse-time throw, s9 schema failure, generation-time coded failure, or gate-time byte-diff — as a red, named `npm test` assertion with a stable code, exactly where every other descriptor-consuming gate already lives; never a green build with wrong bytes.

### 5 · The catalog leg — NO generation, ruled out on three independently-verified grounds; converts to a mechanical agreement gate instead

**Ruled: catalog rows stay hand-authored `@agent-ui/a2ui` content — never generated from the descriptor, under this ADR.** This resolves what this session's own first-pass draft had left as an open, Kim-only fork (deferring "whether/when" catalog generation ever gets built). On the mechanics independently re-verified above (Context), the fork does not survive scrutiny as genuinely open — it resolves to a clear no, on three independent grounds, each sufficient alone:

1. **Curation is authorship, not mirroring.** `Button`'s `label→textContent`, its controller-owned `action`, and its deliberate `size`/`iconOnly` omissions (Context, verified against the real `catalog.json`/`button.ts` pair) are agent-surface DESIGN decisions with no source of truth in the descriptor. Generating the row would require moving that authoring content INTO the descriptor — relocating hand-authored facts to a foreign package's file, removing none of the authorship burden.
2. **Cross-package DAG ownership.** The catalog is `@agent-ui/a2ui`'s contract; descriptors are `@agent-ui/components`' artifact (`CLAUDE.md`'s own DAG, confirmed). A descriptor carrying per-catalog wire curation inverts ownership across the package boundary — conceptually, not just mechanically (no `layering.test.ts` trip-wire catches a data-file inversion, which is exactly why the direction matters here rather than assuming a test would flag it).
3. **The multi-catalog reality.** `a2ui-basic` is upstream-schema-pinned (ADR-0169, re-verified) — permanently ungeneratable from fleet descriptors — and M-D's persona catalogs are compose-time derived (ADR-0172, re-verified, `accepted`). A descriptor-driven row generator would govern exactly one of three catalog shapes the package carries; "cannot drift by construction" would be true of a minority of the surface while reading as a package-wide property.

**What converts instead: a new mechanical agreement gate**, closing the one real drift class the catalog side lacks a check for today (confirmed: `default/index.test.ts`'s SPEC-N2 gate checks COVERAGE — which types have a row at all — never per-prop type/enum agreement). The gate asserts every default-catalog `PropDef` whose `mapsTo` names a descriptor-declared attribute of the mapped control agrees with the descriptor on type kind and enum members (the descriptor being, post-inversion, the props ground truth for that control). Curated OMISSIONS stay legal by construction (a subset is the point, per ground 1); `mapsTo` targets outside the props surface (the 16 verified divergences — `textContent`, controller-owned `action`, value-mark plumbing) are outside the gate's domain, enumerated as such in the gate itself; any deliberate narrowing (a catalog enum tighter than the control's) enters a recorded per-prop exception table with a reason — ADR-0087's include-or-recorded-exclusion law (confirmed, its own title: "gate-encoded exclusion allowlist"), applied one level down. This closes silent catalog-enum drift without moving authorship.

**Why this is a genuine ruling, not a manufactured one.** Every fact underneath it — the `catalog.json`/`button.ts` pair, the DAG statement in `CLAUDE.md`, ADR-0169's upstream pin, ADR-0172's compose-time-derivation ruling — was independently verified at its source in this same session, not taken from either merged draft's prose on faith (one citation IN the parallel draft, "seven `DRIFT_*` codes," did NOT survive this same scrutiny and was corrected in Context rather than propagated — proof the verification was real, not rubber-stamping). A fork this well-supported by in-tree mechanics belongs in Decision, not left open as a false Kim-call; manufacturing open-endedness where the mechanics already resolve the question would be exactly the "process that reads as rigor without being rigor" failure this planning seat's own charter warns against.

### 6 · Migration — gold-first, wave-by-wave, allowlist-drained

Conversion is incremental, never big-bang: `ui-button` converts first (the fleet's reference control, ADR-0004's own precedent, and — per Context — a control with NO bespoke codec, a clean grammar-widening test case), proving grammar + generator + drift gate + s10 retirement on one control before the fleet drains in waves. A seeded exclusion allowlist (control → reason, the ADR-0087 shape) carries any control the cl.2 grammar cannot yet express — its exact known seed population at start is the 7 bespoke-codec controls verified in Context (`table`/`bar-chart`/`sparkline`/`stat`/`sandbox-frame`/`ladder`/`ramp`). Each wave either widens the grammar (a recorded amendment to cl.2's table) or drains an entry. **Done =** the allowlist is empty, every control's props table imports its generated module, every per-control s10 assertion is retired, and the cl.4b fleet drift gate + cl.5 agreement gate are green. Until then, converted and unconverted controls coexist — the driftwire keeps guarding the unconverted half, never both absent for the same control (cl.4c).

**Why not a big-bang rewrite across all 60 (rejected).** Each control's descriptor+test pair is independently shaped today (`table-descriptor.test.ts`'s three-layer LLD-C9 pattern is denser than a plain control's); a fleet-wide flag day risks a coverage-gap window across 60 heterogeneous files. The `icons.gen.ts`/llms-full.txt precedents prove the safer per-control ratchet already works at comparable scale without one.

**Why not keep both the old trip-wire and the new generator running forever, per control, as the steady state (rejected as the END state, legitimate only as a one-control transitional canary).** Running both forever defeats the mirror-tax argument motivating the whole intake — the trip-wire's cost IS the second hand-authored surface it compares against; once generation exists, keeping that surface hand-authored just to keep comparing re-introduces the tax it was meant to remove.

## Non-goals (recorded, not silent)

- **No generator, grammar field, or gate is built here** — this is the design intake GH #483 asked for; a future SPEC/LLD authors against these clauses.
- **No descriptor `.md`, `static props` block, or catalog row changes in this change** — docs-only.
- **No catalog-row generation, ever, under this ADR** (cl.5) — a future ADR would have to supersede cl.5 explicitly to reopen it.
- **No change to `a2ui-basic` (ADR-0169) or the M-D composition architecture (ADR-0172).**
- **No site/llms/dogfood pipeline change** — that half of the inversion shipped long ago and is cited as precedent, not rebuilt.
- **No behavior generation** — `.ts` bodies, traits, CSS, and every s11-checked fact's source remain hand-authored, permanently.
- **No scheduling.** GH #483's own Provenance: *"Not scheduled — an intake candidate for a future wave."*

## Consequences

- The per-widening mirror tax drops from 3-4 hand edits to one descriptor edit + one regeneration (+ a catalog row edit only when the agent surface deliberately widens, now caught by cl.5's agreement gate if it drifts); descriptor↔props drift becomes a build failure with a one-command fix instead of a post-hoc test forensic.
- The descriptor grammar grows exactly three fields (cl.2, sized against the verified populations above) and s9 hardens into a generation input gate — the controlled-YAML subset stays zero-dep, parsed by the one existing reader.
- ~53 controls (60 minus the 7-control bespoke-codec allowlist seed) are the props-generation target population; ~60 committed `{name}.props.gen.ts` files eventually join the per-control folder convention as the allowlist drains — `file-set.test.ts` and the authoring skill's scaffold step must learn the member (Repairs).
- The catalog leg is SETTLED as never-generating (cl.5) — a future SPEC inherits a clear no rather than an open ambition question, but gains a genuinely new drift class closed (the agreement gate) independent of the props-side conversion's own timeline.
- **Negative, accepted:** YAML frontmatter becomes load-bearing runtime source — a typo'd enum member flows into the generated codec and typechecks consistently-wrong. Mitigations are real but not free: the s9 input gate, `tsc` over the generated file, and the existing per-control behavior tests (which assert rendered outcomes, not table shape) — the same trust already extended to `icons.gen.ts`'s vendored table.
- **Negative, accepted:** contributors gain a regeneration step to learn; a red drift gate must state the regenerate command in its failure message (the `llms.test.ts` remediation-comment convention, reused).
- `.claude/skills/agent-ui-component-packaging`'s "one parser, two consumers" note (trip-wire + site) needs a forward-pointer at minimum on ratification — the parser gains a third live consumer on build (the new generator), on top of the props trip-wire and the site; `dogfood-inventory.ts` consumes the same descriptor DATA but through its own independent reader (Context), a standing precedent worth the skill noting explicitly rather than silently counting as a third parser consumer it isn't.

## Open forks

*(The catalog-generation question this session's first-pass draft had left open here — "whether/when catalog-PropDef generation is built at all" — is now RESOLVED into Decision cl.5, on the three independently-verified grounds above. It is not carried forward as an open fork.)*

- **OF1 — Codec-identity closure for the 7 bespoke-codec controls.** Does the descriptor grammar eventually widen with a `codec:` import-naming field (letting `table`/`bar-chart`/`sparkline`/`stat`/`sandbox-frame`/`ladder`/`ramp` fully generate too), or are they ruled a PERMANENT, named escape hatch (never generated, forever hand-authored + s10-guarded)? No in-tree precedent settles this — a real cost/benefit call (a `codec:` field is a bigger, more invasive grammar change than cl.2's three fields) genuinely Kim's to make, not derivable from the mechanics alone. Flagged for a future SPEC, to be decided against the real allowlist population once migration is underway.
- **OF2 — Per-prop teaching carriage.** Whether descriptor attribute commentary rides a structured `description:` field (emitted into the generated file, machine-carried) or stays `#` comments (human-only, never emitted). Both preserve one home; a build-wave LLD decides against real fleet content.
- **OF3 — Sequencing of the cl.5 agreement gate relative to the props-layer conversion.** The agreement gate is independent of props conversion (it checks catalog-vs-descriptor, both of which exist today, unconditionally) — land it standalone as its own hardening ahead of wave 1, or bundle it with the wave. A scope call for a future build-wave's decomp, not resolved here.
- **OF4 — Post-drain fate of the s10 machinery.** Once the fleet fully converts (cl.6's allowlist empties, assuming OF1 eventually resolves to full closure), `compareDescriptorToProps` becomes structurally-redundant code (cl.4c). Delete it, or keep it as a belt-and-braces invariant over the generated files? Deletion is the KISS default; keeping it alive needs a reason recorded at drain time, not assumed here.

## Alternatives considered

- **Full inversion — catalog rows generate from the descriptor too (the issue's maximal reading)** — rejected on cl.5's three independently-verified grounds: curation content would be relocated, not removed; the package DAG's ownership inverts; and two of three catalog shapes (`a2ui-basic`, M-D-derived) can never participate, hollowing "cannot drift by construction" to a minority of the surface.
- **Leave the catalog question as an open, Kim-only fork (this session's own original framing)** — superseded here: the mechanics resolve it cleanly enough (three independent, each-sufficient grounds, all re-verified) that leaving it open would be under-claiming what the record actually supports, not appropriate deference.
- **The reverse direction — generate the descriptor's `attributes[]` FROM the live props table (code→doc)** — rejected: it forks one frontmatter fence into a generated half and a hand half (the exact in-place-region-regeneration vehicle cl.3 rejects for the SAME reason), leaves the mirror tax on the wire/catalog side untouched, and demotes the descriptor from ADR-0004's contract back to a report — the descriptor is also the information-richer document (carries `aria`/`keyboard`/`geometry`/`customStates`/`parts`/prose with no source anywhere else), so only descriptor-as-source is coherent either way.
- **Type-layer-only generation (the issue's own parenthetical, "or its type layer")** — rejected: the drift the gates actually catch is VALUE drift (`DRIFT_DEFAULT`/`DRIFT_REFLECT`/`DRIFT_VALUES`, three of s10's six codes) as much as type drift; generating only types kills the smallest slice of the drift class and keeps most of the tax.
- **Runtime inversion — parse the descriptor at runtime and build props from it** — rejected: ships parser + descriptor bytes in every bundle (the size-budget discipline), makes the runtime depend on doc files, and trades a build-time failure for a runtime one. Build-time projection keeps the zero-dep, self-defining control contract byte-identical for consumers.
- **Ship all three of the issue's named artifacts simultaneously in one build** — rejected: the three targets have genuinely different readiness (site needs nothing; props needs one grammar widening; catalog is now ruled out entirely) — bundling would have either stalled the tractable props target behind a question the mechanics resolve on their own, or rushed that resolution to avoid blocking it.
- **Big-bang rewrite of all 60 descriptors' generation status in one wave** — rejected in cl.6: real coverage-gap risk across 60 heterogeneous files; the `icons.gen.ts`/llms-full.txt precedents prove the safer per-control ratchet already works at comparable scale.
- **Do nothing — keep the trip-wires** — rejected with respect: the gates demonstrably work (six drift codes, fleet-proven across ~60 `*-descriptor.test.ts` files), but they fire AFTER the fact, and the ADR-0161/0163/0169 arc shows the mirror tax is recurring, not incidental. GH #483's premise is confirmed by the record.
