- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/switch/switch.css:72: shot indicators__switch-demo__1000__light.png (Security row, "Disabled off", "Disabled on"; same in __400__dark). A disabled switch loses its track: the track goes to neutral-surface-high, almost the page colour, and the thumb goes dark (on-surface-variant). What remains is a lone dark dot that reads as a list bullet, not a switch, and "locked on" looks the same as "locked off". Keep the track visible: an outline-variant track (border or fill) and a thumb in a muted role that is never darker than the enabled thumb. Measurable: disabled track against its surface at least 1.3:1, and disabled thumb contrast no higher than the enabled thumb's.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/button/button.css:88: shot actions__button-demo__1000__light.png and __400__dark.png ("Publish", disabled solid primary). The disabled primary turns into a neutral grey pill with full-strength dark ink and its icon. It reads as an enabled neutral button and carries more weight than the enabled "Save draft" beside it, so the gate looks open. Keep the variant's silhouette and drop the emphasis: a low-emphasis container plus low-emphasis ink (the 12% container and 38% content pattern), so a disabled button is always quieter than every enabled sibling. Measurable: a disabled button's ink contrast is at most that of the quietest enabled button in the same row.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/checkbox/checkbox.css:70: shot indicators__checkbox-demo__1000__light.png ("Read documents", "Disabled", "Disabled, checked"). On a disabled checkbox the box border and fill both go to neutral-surface-high, so the box disappears. Unchecked becomes nothing at all, and checked becomes a bare dark tick with no box. radio.css:72 has the same root cause the other way round: a disabled checked radio becomes a solid dark disc with no ring (indicators__radio-demo__1000__light.png "Disabled, checked"), darker than the enabled checked radio. Use one fleet disabled recipe: keep the outline at outline-variant and mute the mark below its enabled contrast. This is the same recipe as the switch and button findings above, and toggle.css:93 belongs to it too. Measurable: the same two contrast rules as the switch finding, applied to every indicator.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/badge/badge.css:105: shot atoms__badge-demo__1000__light.png ("Count pills + the empty-label floor"; __400__dark too). Count badges "3", "12" and "99+" stretch to the full card width as long bars, and the "empty-label floor: a dot" renders as a 120px grey bar. The host is inline-flex, but its parent `.demo-figure` (site/pages/containers.css:44) is a flex column with the default stretch. Any column parent (cards, an A2UI Column) will stretch it the same way. A badge is a display atom, not a fill control: add `max-inline-size: max-content` (stretch respects the max), so it hugs everywhere. Measurable: a rendered ui-badge's inline-size is no more than its max-content plus 1px.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/icon/icon.css:23: shot atoms__icon-demo__1000__light.png ("Sizing: the ambient font-size ramp", "Beside list rows", "Meaningful icons"). All five ramp specimens, from 0.875rem to 3rem, render the same ~10px icon. The file-row icons and status icons are set at 1.25rem and 1.5rem but render at about 10px, smaller than the text beside them. foundations.gen.css:28 defines `--md-sys-icon-box` on `:root`, so the `1em` fallback never applies. Choose one: either an icon tracks ambient font-size outside a control cell (scope icon-box to explicit `[data-tier]` regions or to control cells), or 2n is the rule. If 2n wins, repair the stale record in the same change (site/pages/icon-demo.ts:84 prose, the ramp section and icon.md). Measurable: a standalone ui-icon leading a text row is at least 0.875 times that row's font-size.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/text-field/text-field.css:74: shots entry__text-field-demo__400__dark.png, entry__combo-box-demo__400__dark.png, entry__select-demo__400__dark.png and entry__otp-field-demo__400__dark.png. - packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/text-field/text-field.css:298: shot entry__text-field-demo__1000__light.png ("25" vs "€ 0.00" and "0"; also textarea "Ticket auto-triaged…" and text-field "VND-00421" readonly). The filled field reads as hovered or selected, and readonly values read as heavier than the editable fields. Meanwhile the idle value ink equals the placeholder ink (:79, :91), so "0.00" cannot be told from a placeholder. Keep one idle fill for empty and filled, and separate value from placeholder by ink alone (value at on-surface, placeholder at neutral). Measurable: sibling fields in idle state share one background, and value ink contrast is greater than placeholder ink contrast.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/otp-field/otp-field.css:44: shot entry__otp-field-demo__1000__light.png (all specimens). At h/2 the square cells become circles. Recommend a round square (radius n, squircle) for square entry cells. Measurable: an interactive entry cell with aspect ratio about 1 has radius below w/2.
