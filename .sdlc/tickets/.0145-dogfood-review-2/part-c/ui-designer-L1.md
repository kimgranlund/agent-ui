New findings, most visible first:
1. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/table/table.css:85: data__table-demo__400__dark.png. At 400 the full-capability and single-selection tables show only Account, Plan and Region. Seats, MRR and Signed up are cut off at the column edge with no scrollbar, fade or shadow, so the table looks complete and the money columns seem not to exist. Cause: `[data-part='scroll'] { overflow-x: auto }` with overlay scrollbars and no edge cue. ui-card-content already ships the fleet's edge-fade mask for exactly this. Fix: give the table's scroll part the same scroll-driven inline-end fade (or an inset end shadow) while `scrollLeft < scrollWidth - clientWidth`. Measurable: when the scroll part's scrollWidth > clientWidth, its inline-end edge paints a computed `mask-image` or `box-shadow` other than none.
2. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/stat/stat.css:150: data__stat-demo__1000__light.png, Ring variant. The four sibling rings come out at three diameters: "72%" and "18%" about 94px, "47 / 50" about 120px, "pending" about 126px. So a 2x2 grid of identical tiles has rings that jump in size, and the bigger ring reads as the more important metric. Cause: the T-0141 fix sizes the ring from its own figure (`auto` track, `justify-self: stretch`, `min-inline-size: ring-size` at stat.css:133-158), so each tile's ring tracks the width of its own text. Fix: keep the ring at a fixed `--ui-stat-ring-size` and fit the figure to the hole instead. Make the ring an inline-size container and give the value `font-size: min(<heading size>, <hole size> / <figure ch count>)` with cqi units, or step the figure down to the title row when it is wider than the hole. Measurable: sibling `[variant=ring]` stats in one row or grid have equal ring diameters within 1px, and each value box still fits the hole.
3. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/gauge/gauge.css:132: charts__gauge-demo__1000__light.png, all three sections. The rings sit at the start edge and the legend hugs the far end edge, about 420px away, with dead space between. The legend no longer reads as the key for those rings, worst in the zero-padding card, where the two sit at opposite corners. Cause: `[data-part='legend'] { inline-size: 100%; align-items: flex-end }` pins the legend to the box's end edge however wide the box gets. Fix: above the 28em container breakpoint, lay out the rings and legend as one start-aligned group (`grid-template-columns: auto auto; justify-content: start; column-gap: <group gutter>`), or centre the pair. Keep the full-overlay only while the box is near square. Measurable: the inline gap between the rings svg's painted circle and the legend's nearest edge is at most 2 x the widget margin.
4. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/_chart/chart-axis.css:66: charts__column-chart-demo__1000__light.png and __400__dark.png. The two stacked series ("Product", "Services") are adjacent ramp steps (primary-300 against primary-400 in light, primary-200 against primary-300 in dark), so each column reads as one bar with a faint seam, worst in dark, where both are pale lavender. The pale dark-mode bars also swamp the neutral tick chips (pass-1 status above). Cause: series 1 and 2 are one ramp step apart (chart-axis.css:66-67). Fix: separate neighbouring series by at least two ramp steps, or alternate the hue family (primary, then secondary or tertiary). In dark, drop series 1 to a mid step so the chips and gridlines stay visible over it. Measurable: adjacent series inks differ by at least 1.5:1 luminance contrast (the dataviz floor for touching fills), and each series against the surface is at least 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11).
5. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/bar-chart/bar-chart.css:78: charts__bar-chart-demo__1000__light.png and __400__dark.png, "Mixed sign" and the all-negative degenerate tile. Diverging bars hang off an invisible origin: the grey track runs the full width and the fill starts mid-track, so "Churn -2.7" looks like a short bar floating left of nowhere, and there is no visible zero to read signs against. Cause: the track is one undivided rail (bar-chart.css:78-84), and no rule draws the zero position the fill math already has (`--_bar-start`, bar-chart.ts). Fix: when any value is negative, paint a 1px (`--md-sys-c`) on-surface-variant zero rule across the rows at the origin. Use a `::before` on the track at `inset-inline-start: <origin>%`, or a background stop. Optionally shade the negative half of the track one step darker. Measurable: in a mixed-sign bar chart, a visible mark of at least 3:1 against the track sits at the origin x of every row.
6. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/calendar/calendar.css:176: data__calendar-demo__1000__light.png. Every calendar panel floats centred in the content column, while the section's heading, intro paragraph and the "Selected: (none)" and "Validity:" captions under it start at the column edge. The panel's left edge sits about 175px in from every other block on the page. Cause: `margin-inline: auto` on the capped panel. Fix: `margin-inline-end: auto` (start-align, the flow every other block follows), and leave centring to a consumer who wants it. Measurable: the calendar panel's inline-start x equals its section heading's inline-start x within 1px.
7. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/gauge/gauge.css:124: charts__gauge-demo__1000__light.png ("Degenerate data", `data="[]"`) and charts__column-chart-demo__1000__light.png ("Degenerate data", left tile). The empty gauge and the empty column chart paint a blank 200px box, and the empty bar chart paints nothing at all. On the sparkline page the same case draws a deliberate dashed baseline (sparkline.css:47-48). Four chart controls give three different answers to "no data", and the blank boxes read as failed renders. Cause: only sparkline has an empty rule. gauge.css, column-chart.css and bar-chart.css have none. Fix: one fleet empty treatment per chart: the gauge draws its outer track ring at 0%, the column chart draws its plot box with the baseline gridline, and the bar chart draws one track row, each at track ink. Measurable: every chart host with zero data paints at least one visible mark (computed non-transparent stroke or background) inside its box.
8. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/timeline-item/timeline-item.css:168: data__timeline-demo__1000__light.png and __400__dark.png. The current step "Shipped" is a small blue dot about half the diameter of the green done discs above it, so the most important step is the weakest mark in the rail. Cause: the active dot reads `--ui-timeline-item-dot-size` (the 2n - 2c glyph box, timeline-item.css:63 and 168-169), while the done disc fills the icon box (`--ui-timeline-item-icon-size`, timeline-item.css:256-262). Fix: draw the active marker at the disc's outer diameter, either as a ring of that size with the dot inside or as a filled primary disc, so every status marker shares one outer size and shape carries the state. Measurable: all marker outer diameters in one ui-timeline are equal within 1px.
9. site/pages/timeline-demo.ts:40: data__timeline-demo__1000__light.png and __400__dark.png. The "Append the next step" button sits about 2px under "Shipped", so it reads as part of the last timeline row and not as a separate action. Cause: `exampleSection(..., timeline, addStep)` stacks the button directly after the timeline with no flow gap. The `prose-gap` lint only covers a `p`. Fix: wrap the action in the page's action row, or give block siblings in an example section the widget margin. Measurable: extend `prose-gap` so any block (not only `p`) under a specimen sits at least n below it.
10. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/calendar/calendar.css:78: data__calendar-demo__1000__light.png, "Range constraint". The disabled days (July 1-4 and 21-31) and the adjacent-month days (June 28-30, August 1-8) render in the same grey, so a person cannot tell "you may not pick this" from "this belongs to another month and is clickable". Cause: `--ui-calendar-disabled-ink: neutral-on-surface-variant` and `--ui-calendar-outside-ink: neutral` (calendar.css:78-81, applied at 434-435) land on near-identical greys. Fix: give disabled days a second, non-colour cue (a strike-through, or no hover and a dimmer ink than outside days), and keep outside days a step brighter. Measurable: a disabled in-month cell and an outside cell differ in computed `text-decoration` or by at least 1.3:1 ink contrast.
11. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/pagination/pagination.ts:143: navigation__pagination-demo__1000__light.png, Standalone. Every stop ("Previous", "1", "4", "6", "12", "Next") is primary-ink text, so the pager reads as a run of inline links, and the current page is only a faint tonal pill. The leading "Previous" text also starts about 7px inside the section heading's edge (ghost button padding). Fix: give the number stops neutral on-surface ink (primary only on the current page's soft pill, or on hover), and pull the first stop out by its own padding (`margin-inline-start: calc(-1 * padding-inline)`) so the text aligns with the heading. Measurable: non-current number stops compute a neutral ink, and the first stop's text x equals the heading x within 1px.
12. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/breadcrumb/breadcrumb.css:41 (nit): navigation__breadcrumb-demo__1000__light.png. With `--ui-breadcrumb-gap: space-xs`, "Home/Docs/Components/Breadcrumb" sits about 3px either side of each separator and reads as one slashed token. The slotted "→" variant is just as tight. Fix: step the gap to the c or group-gutter level the ladder assigns between inline siblings, about 6 to 8px. Measurable: the separator-to-crumb gap is at least c.
13. packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/bar-chart/bar-chart.css:44 (nit): charts__bar-chart-demo__400__dark.png, "all zero" tile (and every unfilled track in dark). The neutral-container track sits barely above the dark card fill, so "Refunds 0 / Chargebacks 0" shows labels and zeros with no visible bar row, which looks like a missing chart. Fix: in dark, use the next higher container step for the track, or add a 1px outline-variant edge. Measurable: track against its surface is at least 1.3:1 (the `disabled-edge` floor).
