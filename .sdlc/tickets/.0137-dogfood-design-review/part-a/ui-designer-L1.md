- packages/agent-ui/app/src/controls/conversation/conversation-composer.ts:859: at 400px the composer toolbar shows two blank grey circles (the first and fourth buttons), so it looks broken (shots/shell__a2ui-chat__400__dark.png, __400__light.png). - packages/agent-ui/a2ui/src/catalog/a2ui-basic/factories.ts:198: two cards stacked in a Basic Column touch with no gap, and the second card's top border lands on the first card's bottom border (shots/a2ui__BookingForm__1000__light.png, a2ui__ContrastPair__1000__light.png and both 400 dark). The upstream Basic Column has no gap property (a2ui-basic/catalog.json Column), and `rowColumnFactory` creates a bare ui-column, whose default gap is `none` (components/src/controls/column/column.css:40), so a Basic Column can never space its children: give Basic Row and Column a default `gap` at create time, the widget gutter (`md` on the canvas register). Measurable: adjacent bordered siblings inside a ui-column or ui-row are never closer than n (gap 0 between two cards fails).
- packages/agent-ui/a2ui/src/catalog/personas/croupier/factories.ts:310: at 400px the CardTable action row splits. Hit stays beside "Bankroll 1,000 · Bet 50" and Stand and Double wrap to a new line, half out of view (shots/a2ui__CardTable__400__dark.png, __400__light.png). The card is one `flex-wrap: wrap` box (:310-317), the readout is `flex: 1 1 auto` (:365), and each Button is a loose flex item, so the row wraps one button at a time: put the action Buttons in their own row element that wraps as a unit, or give the readout `flex: 1 0 100%` under a narrow container. Measurable: ui-buttons that are siblings in one action group share one top edge, or all wrap together (a split group fails).
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/button/button.css:88: a disabled button reads as an enabled neutral button, with near-full-contrast ink on a pale pill. In the QuestionCard, "Check answer" looks clickable (shots/a2ui__QuestionCard__1000__light.png, __400__dark.png), and on the button doc "Disabled" looks like a third variant (shots/shell__button-doc__1000__light.png). `--ui-button-ink` is `neutral-on-surface-variant`, the same ink the badge and labels use: repoint the disabled ink to `--md-sys-color-neutral-on-surface-disabled` and the fill to `--md-sys-color-neutral-container-disabled` (both exist in shared/src/tokens/tokens.css:60,72). Measurable: a [disabled] ui-button's label contrast against its fill is at most 3:1 and lower than the same variant enabled.
- packages/agent-ui/components/src/controls/product-card/product-card.css:232: the "Best rate" badge over the offer image loses its pill. The dot and text sit directly on the blue gradient and the light-mode text is hard to read (shots/a2ui__product-card-demo__1000__light.png; dark is legible but still has no visible pill). The overlay keeps the tag fill `neutral-container` (badge.css:59), which does not read over a photo: over an image, repoint `--ui-badge-fill` to an opaque surface (`neutral-surface`) in the overlay rule. Measurable: a badge positioned over a `[data-part=image]` has an opaque fill (alpha 1) and its ink-to-fill contrast is at least 4.5:1.
- site/pages/containers.css:44: on the product-card demo the specimen frame's corner (0.6rem) is tighter than the product card inside it (card level, about 16px) at 1rem padding, so the nested corners read inverted (shots/a2ui__product-card-demo__1000__light.png, __400__dark.png). `.demo-figure` hand-tables `border-radius: 0.6rem`, `padding: 1rem` and `gap: 0.5rem`: derive them from the levels, with radius = inner card radius + padding (the concentric rule, ADR-0245 Amendment 5 item 3) and padding = `--md-sys-card-margin`. Measurable: for a bordered box holding a bordered child, the outer radius is at least the inner radius plus the inset (outer smaller than inner fails).
- site/pages/containers.css:20: the explanatory notes under the specimens sit flush against what is above them. Under the Dungeon card, "The sign is text..." starts 0px below its border; "done is the neutral outcome..." and "No stats and no children..." touch their cards; and the "Win hand" and "Toggle win and loss" buttons touch the paragraph below (shots/a2ui__game-hud-demo__1000__light.png, a2ui__result-card-demo__1000__light.png, both 400 dark). `section > p` is `margin: 0 0 1rem`, styled as a lead-in above a specimen, and these pages use it as a note below one (site/pages/game-hud-demo.ts:48, site/pages/result-card-demo.ts:52): give a p that follows a non-text sibling a top margin of the widget gutter, or lay the section out as a flex column with a gap. Measurable: the vertical gap between a specimen or button box and the next text block is at least n.
- packages/agent-ui/app/src/controls/agent-admin/agent-admin.css:663: each model row ends in two unlabeled controls, a switch and a radio, with no column header, so a person cannot tell "include" from "default".
