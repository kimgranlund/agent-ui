Heads-up hold'em against the dealer: two hole cards each, then a shared flop (three cards), turn, and river, with a betting round before each street — check, bet, call, raise, or fold, staked from the running chip count. Best five-card hand from the seven wins the pot, standard rankings (high card up to royal flush). Reveal both hands honestly at showdown.
