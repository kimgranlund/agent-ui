Blackjack on a 48-card Spanish deck — every 10-spot removed, faces stay. A player 21 ALWAYS wins, and player blackjack beats dealer blackjack. Bonus payouts for a five-plus-card 21 and for 6-7-8 or 7-7-7; double-after-split and late surrender allowed. Deal and settle as blackjack otherwise.
