British blackjack: BOTH dealer cards stay face-down and the dealer wins all ties. Twist = hit, stick = stand (only on 15 or better), buy = double the stake for a face-down card (repeatable). Pontoon (Ace + ten-card) pays 2:1; a five-card trick (five cards, 21 or under) beats everything but pontoon.
